📐 Algebra

Square of a Sum

Today's magical formula ✨

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
Square of the sum of two numbers

💡 Simple Explanation

The square of a sum is equal to the square of the first number, plus twice their product, plus the square of the second number.

📝 Example Calculation

Example: (2 + 3)²
= 2² + 2 × 2 × 3 + 3²
= 4 + 12 + 9
= 25 🎉
